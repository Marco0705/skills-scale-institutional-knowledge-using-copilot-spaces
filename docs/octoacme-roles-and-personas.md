# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX Designer

### Role Summary
UX Designers research user needs, design intuitive interfaces, and validate that solutions are usable and accessible. They collaborate with Product and Engineering to ensure user-centered design.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Ensure accessibility standards (WCAG) and design consistency
- Collaborate with developers on implementation and design QA
- Iterate designs based on feedback and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize product usability and user satisfaction
- Reduce friction and improve conversion rates
- Maintain a consistent, accessible design system

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and critique sessions
- User research findings and personas
- Figma/Sketch files and design handoff documentation
- Usability testing reports

---

## QA Lead

### Role Summary
QA Leads define testing strategy, coordinate quality assurance activities, and ensure releases meet quality standards. They work closely with developers and product teams to prevent defects and validate acceptance criteria.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and coordinate test execution
- Review acceptance criteria and create test plans
- Coordinate automated and manual testing efforts
- Identify, track, and triage bugs and quality risks
- Lead smoke testing and release validation

### Goals
- Ensure releases meet quality and acceptance criteria
- Minimize production defects and customer-impacting bugs
- Improve test coverage and automation maturity

### Typical Communication
- Test plans and bug triage meetings
- Release readiness reviews and sign-offs
- Quality metrics dashboards and defect reports
- Test case documentation and QA checklists

---

## DevOps/Platform Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps/Platform Engineers build and maintain CI/CD pipelines, infrastructure, and deployment automation. They enable reliable, secure, and efficient software delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines and automation
- Manage infrastructure, environments, and deployment tooling
- Monitor system reliability, performance, and security
- Implement and support observability and incident response
- Collaborate on deployment strategies and rollback procedures

### Goals
- Reduce deployment cycle time and risk
- Maintain high system reliability and uptime
- Enable developer self-service and fast feedback loops

### Typical Communication
- Runbooks and incident post-mortems
- Infrastructure and pipeline documentation
- Deployment notifications and release coordination
- Platform roadmap and tooling updates

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge business stakeholders and technical teams by defining requirements, analyzing processes, and ensuring solutions align with business goals. They facilitate requirement gathering and validate that delivered features meet business needs.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document business requirements and user stories
- Analyze business processes and identify improvement opportunities
- Facilitate workshops and stakeholder alignment sessions
- Validate that solutions meet business objectives and acceptance criteria
- Support change management and user adoption

### Goals
- Ensure delivered solutions address true business needs
- Reduce rework by clarifying requirements early
- Improve cross-functional understanding and alignment

### Typical Communication
- Requirements documents and user story refinement
- Process flow diagrams and stakeholder workshops
- Acceptance criteria validation and UAT coordination
- Business impact analysis and change management plans
